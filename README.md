# Fiber & Scalpels — Inside the Algorithmic Hospital (2019–2026)

An interactive educational simulation exploring the economics of **Surgical Robotics**, **Clinical Artificial Intelligence**, and **Dedicated Broadband Infrastructure (*Sanità Connessa* PNRR 1–10 Gbps)** in public hospitals.

## How to Play
- Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser (desktop or mobile), or visit the hosted **GitHub Pages** link.
- Choose between a **Provincial Capital Hospital** (*Main Sample: 213 hospitals*) and a **Peripheral Hospital** (*Step 7 Subsample: 164 non-capital hospitals*).
- Walk through the hospital corridor using the **Arrow Keys** (`⬆️ ⬇️ ⬅️ ➡️` or `WASD` / touch controls) and unlock all **5 wards sequentially**:
  1. **Robotic Operating Room (2019–2021)** — *Da Vinci Steady-Hand Surgery* & surgeon skill counterfactuals.
  2. **AI Radiology & Diagnostics (2021–2022)** — *AI Data Snake*, clinical data appropriability, and inter-hospital research networks.
  3. **Server Room & Sanità Connessa (2023–2024)** — *10 Gbps Fiber Pipe Puzzle* & measuring actual hospital connectivity vs. street-level fiber.
  4. **3D AR Convergence & AGENAS Lab (2025)** — *Patents & HTA Breakout*, national procurement vs. HTA, and surgical telemetry governance.
  5. **Grand Lecture Hall (2026)** — *Turn-Based Peer-Review Duel* against **Referee #2** on staggered Difference-in-Differences, placebo outcomes, and external validity.
- Press **`G`** anytime in the corridor for a field briefing from your scientific mentor, or **`T`** to open the synchronized **Lecture Theory & Econometric Method Boxes**.
